## How to use the images

Study relationships rather than surface decoration:

- approximate evidence-band share and torn-paper behavior;
- how the middle print retains count, rhythm, axes, and partial silhouettes;
- how the lower ink changes scale and material without becoming unrelated fantasy;
- how source color travels through the paper field;
- how empty ivory paper balances the dense region.

The gallery records successful visual tendencies, not mandatory motifs. Do not copy an example's subject, caption, seal, signature placement, crosshairs, or incidental marks into a new work. Follow the current rules in `SKILL.md`, `art-direction.md`, and `quality-gate.md` whenever an older example conflicts with them. In particular, omit unreadable generated text and do not add calligraphy or seals merely because they appear in a reference.
