# Quality Gate

Inspect the final raster at full size and thumbnail size.

## Source lock

- The upper evidence is a direct crop/scale of the supplied source, not a generated approximation.
- The primary evidence, count, geometry, viewpoint, light, optical depth, and photographic accidents remain recognizable.
- At least 90% of important evidence is unobstructed.
- The crop is informative; no anchor was lost merely to center the image.
- The torn boundary is one irregular fibrous event, not a straight divider, scallop, smooth vector wave, or four-sided frame.
- Run `scripts/verify_evidence.py` when the deterministic compositor was used.

## Information decay

- Top: high semantic information and photographic specificity.
- Middle: medium semantic information, high structural intelligence, incomplete objects, and one controlled spatial impossibility.
- Bottom: low literal information, high material information, and an atmospheric residue still connected to the source.
- The three states are distinguished by information and material, not boxes.

## Printed memory

- Contains roughly three to seven meaningful source fragments.
- Reads dry, printed, broken, analytical, and incomplete.
- Preserves relationships rather than redrawing a complete scene.
- The surreal event changes the behavior of real evidence and introduces no unrelated fantasy.
- At first glance it is abstract; at second glance the source becomes mentally reconstructible.

## Ink afterimage

- Contains two to five broad masses and only necessary gestures.
- Reads wet, chromatic, expanded, absorbent, and lower-frequency than the middle.
- At least half the region reads as untouched or very lightly marked paper.
- Black remains subordinate; source-derived color is materially present.
- It is not a miniature watercolor version of the photograph.

## Material collision

- At least one wet relationship rises into the printed field and changes material.
- At least one printed relationship descends or dissolves.
- Occlusion, dot loss, absorption, edge bleed, pigment pooling, or texture transfer makes the collision believable.
- There is no empty horizontal gap or precise middle/bottom boundary.
- Any photo overlap is peripheral, approximately 2–8%, and does not cover the anchor.

## Surface, color, and text

- The sheet is matte warm ivory, tactile, restrained, and flat.
- The dominant accent and supporting hues come from the source.
- No competing saturated palette, dirty parchment, heavy grunge, glossy mockup, or generic decorative marks.
- No unrelated mountains, bamboo, cranes, temples, celestial objects, portals, fantasy architecture, calligraphy, or seal.
- All text is exact. `Starryear` is spelled correctly and visually secondary.

## Stopping rule

If source lock fails, fix the deterministic assembly rather than regenerating the photograph. If print/ink structure fails, revise only those failed invariants and regenerate the non-photographic field once. After the second attempt, deliver the stronger result and state any remaining limitation.
